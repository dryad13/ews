Welcome To East Wind Shipping !!

EastWind is a well known name in the Shipping Industry of Pakistan and within the circles abroad where Pakistan trade takes place. Since its inception, EastWind Shipping has gained the confidence of its Principals and Customers for the Skill level, Professionalism and Experience of its Crew.

EastWind delivers through the vast experience of its key personnel and this is the reason that today EastWind is handling the following wide range of commodities and ships.

Tankers carrying liquid cargoes including Chemicals, Molasses, Ethanol and Petroleum products
Break Bulk vessels carrying commodities consisting of Steel products, General and Project cargo
Dry Bulk Ships with cargoes such as Grains / Seeds, Fertilizers, Shredded Scrap and Coal
Container Feeder vessels, represent Container Feeder Market Leader – Global Feeder Shipping LLC
Demo ships' handling at Gadani

EastWind also provide Empty Container Storage, receiving and delivery and Container repair services to Container Main Lines Operators and NVOCCs. We operate from TPX – MT Khan Road only 2 Km away from Port with an area of about 16000 Square Meters and from East Wharf – Keamari – Karachi Port where the facility is spread on an area of about 8000 Square Meters.

EastWind Shipping is part of a Shipping Enterprise having an established Liner Agency - Green Pak Shipping
representing Evergreen Container Line in Pakistan.

Our partners own and manage Global Feeder Shipping LLC - a market leader in Container Feeder industry serving United Arab Emirates, Middle East, Arabian & Upper Arabian Gulf, Indian Subcontinent, South East Asia, and the Far East.
