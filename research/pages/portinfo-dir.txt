Berthing policy: OP-1: is dedicated berth for POL vessels, however, chemical tankers can berth when no POL tanker vessel is around. OP-2: is allocated to tankers on first come first serve basis. OP-3: is allocated to tankers in such a way that after two POL tankers one NON POL tanker ship is accommodated. DB-1: is allocated to tankers on first come first serve basis, if they are able to work at this berth. General Pilotage is compulsory, Pilot station is known as Karachi Pilot Station, position is Lat 24 Deg 44.7 Min North, Long 066 Deg 57.3 Min East. VHF channel used by port control and pilots is channel 16 and 12 respectively.
